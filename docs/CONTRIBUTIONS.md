# Scientific contributions and software provenance

The pre-experimental portfolio-selection project synthesizes **distinct but complementary methods**:

- **Clinton Monteiro** — Gaussian-process-informed output/cost forecasting based on Ginkgo control experiments; grouping by expected shared model error/untested-factor risk; Latin-hypercube probing of model sensitivity; and the original GP-informed Round 1 selection proposal.
- **Kourosh Afshinjoo** — independent recipe-space correlation/coverage analysis; direct candidate-pool auditing, internal/external exact deduplication, controlled-contrast and sweep detection, model-free facility-location/Maximin selection, and model-based-versus-model-free portfolio comparison and visualization.
- **Ronan Chance** — `htgaa-automation` application infrastructure, authenticated data acquisition, and protected deployment environment.

The original external data, competition submissions, and Ginkgo experiments remain the work of their respective contributors and sources. These credits do not imply that the present release reproduces the original Gaussian-process training pipeline or validates any predicted biological response.

For journal publication, authorship order, specific roles, data permissions, and licensing should be agreed among contributors and reported according to the venue's authorship and data-access policies. Names are deliberately not embedded in method identifiers, variable names, route paths, or end-user chart labels.
